Ten Years of Building, Writing, and Selling, Mostly by Hand.

I'm based in Presque Isle, Maine. Most of what I know, I learned by taking on a job that was slightly too big and finishing it anyway: a two-person web agency, a blog that hit 40,000 readers, a car wash sales floor, a hotel site nobody asked me to build. That's most of this page.

How I Got Here

  1. 2015

    Co-founded Ardea Studios

    A two-person web development shop, started with my business partner Luna right as the industry made its hard pivot to mobile-first design. Served around 30 small-business clients doing responsive WordPress builds.

  2. 2016

    Migrated a 2,700-Page Site by Hand

    Took on a full Drupal-to-WordPress migration, moving 2,700+ pages and 3,400+ posts individually into a new Divi build. About 800 hours of work. We finished it, and it's the project that ended the company: the scope cost us the rest of our client list.

  3. 2019

    Founded Streamershaven

    Built a content blog from nothing, writing tutorials on OBS Studio, Twitch setup, and streaming gear. Grew it to 40,000+ monthly visitors through organic SEO alone, no ad spend.

  4. 2021

    Sold Streamershaven

    Sold the site to a new owner and stepped away from active SEO work for a few years.

  5. 2023

    A Year of QA

    Contract QA tester for Systrix Software on AutomatePOD: pre-release smoke testing, test case authoring, and launch readiness. Where most of my testing habits come from.

  6. 2024

    Learned to Close

    Sales associate at Washville Car Wash. A 23.1% conversion rate on unlimited wash memberships, third in the company for sales, 49 sold on opening day, and enough Spanish picked up on the floor to pitch and close in it.

  7. 2025

    Built The Northeastland Hotel and IgnitePI

    Hired as front desk staff, then proposed and built a full custom WordPress rebuild for the hotel's site, while managing rate plans and guest retention on the desk. Alongside it, I built a site for the hotel's nonprofit owner, IgnitePI, on the same theme family. Nobody asked me to. I asked them.

  8. 2026

    LD Maker Co. and Alpha Pressure Washing

    Built the Shopify storefront for a 3D-printing business I co-founded with a friend. Then ran operations and marketing for a pressure-washing company: Facebook Reels that drew thousands of views, and a 2,500-client CRM migration finished in the first month.
