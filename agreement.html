04Optional White-Label Addon

By default, all websites I design include a small credit in the footer that helps me grow as a business. For $300, you can get this credit removed. This is in addition to the package plan, is paid upfront, and is non-refundable. You may request this addon at any time during or after development.

05Revisions

You receive two rounds of revisions included in the package price. To keep the process efficient:

  • Each revision round must be delivered as a single, consolidated list of requested changes (e.g., one email or feedback document).
  • Revisions apply to tweaking copy, colors, spacing, image swaps, or minor element repositioning within the agreed layout. Complete structural redesigns or scope changes after layout approval will be billed separately at $60/hour upon your written authorization.

Review & Approval: You will have 5 business days to review work submitted for initial presentation or revision rounds and provide feedback. If no feedback or approval is provided within 5 business days, the submitted work will be considered approved, and project development will move forward.

06Timeline

Once I have received all necessary assets (photos, logos, written content, brand materials, access credentials), initial design concepts will be completed within 2–3 weeks of the effective date.

  • Client Delays: The production clock does not start until all required materials are received. If asset delivery is delayed by more than 30 consecutive calendar days, the project will be placed on hold, and a restart fee of $150 may apply to resume scheduling.
  • Developer Delays: If development is expected to exceed the 3-week timeframe, I will notify you prior to the deadline to review project status and schedule a progress meeting.
  • Early Completion: If the website is completed ahead of schedule, there's no need to rush, you'll have until the end of the 3-week window (based on the Effective Date in Section 1) to submit the remaining balance. That said, the site won't be transferred to your domain or handed over until the final payment is received in full, regardless of how early the build finishes (see Section 7).

07Who Owns What, and When

Anything you provide me, your logo, photos, written content, brand assets, whatever it is, is and stays yours. I'm just using it to build your site; I never claim ownership over it.

On my end: until your final payment clears, all the website files, code, and original copy I create stay mine. Once that final payment is in, everything I built transfers to you, full ownership, no strings attached.

08Expectations

I can't promise instant results, top search rankings, or a flood of traffic. This agreement covers building the site and the copy on it, that's it. I'm not responsible for how the site performs in search engines or AI results short or long-term.

I can promise that the website will be performance optimized, fully mobile responsive, and that it will be AA WCAG compliant. I also adhere to the AAA WCAG guidelines for contrast ratio to ensure content is easily legible, unless you specifically request a color combination that does not meet that standard.

Advice will be provided upon completion to keep it that way in case you intend to make changes on your own.

Post-Launch Support: I provide a 14-day bug-fix period following site handover to fix any technical issues or broken features that fail to work as agreed in Section 2. Minor design modifications, new feature additions, or updates outside the original scope during or after this period will be billed at $60/hour.

09Getting in Touch

Email and phone (both listed in Section 1) are the best ways to reach me. A couple of ground rules on calls: I won't be answering the phone before 9 AM, after 5 PM, or on weekends. And even during regular hours (9–5, Monday through Friday), I can't guarantee I'll pick up right away, life happens. Email's your best bet for a reliable response.

10Keeping Things Private

Anything confidential either of us shares about our businesses during this project stays between us. Neither of us will share it with anyone else without the other's okay. I'm happy to sign an NDA if necessary.

Portfolio Rights: Unless otherwise agreed in writing via an NDA, I reserve the right to display the completed website design and project details in my professional portfolio, website, and promotional materials after the site is launched. To opt out, either check the box below or request a separate NDA before signing, one of the two is required for your project to be excluded from my portfolio.
