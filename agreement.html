04Optional Discounts

Two ways to bring the price down. Tick whichever apply; each comes off the final payment, and the total above follows.

Changing your mind: either discount can be added or dropped later, and neither direction involves a refund. To add one after signing (keep the credit after all, or send me your finished copy before the production clock starts), the discount comes off the final payment; once that is paid there is nothing to take it from, so the discounts close at final payment. To drop one you took (have the credit removed, or have me write the copy after all), the discount amount becomes due, and once it's paid I remove the credit or write the copy.

A change of mind late in development can mean real rework (copy written into a finished layout, or a footer rebuilt), so if it affects the schedule we agree an extension to the timeline at the time of the decision, before I make the change.

05Revisions

You receive two rounds of revisions included in the package price. To keep the process efficient:

  • Each revision round must be delivered as a single, consolidated list of requested changes (e.g., one email or feedback document).
  • Revisions apply to tweaking copy, colors, spacing, image swaps, or minor element repositioning within the agreed layout. Complete structural redesigns or scope changes after layout approval will be billed separately at $60/hour upon your written authorization.

Review & Approval: You will have 5 business days to review work submitted for initial presentation or revision rounds and provide feedback. If no feedback or approval is provided within 5 business days, the submitted work will be considered approved, and project development will move forward.

07Who Owns What, and When

Anything you provide me, your logo, photos, written content, brand assets, whatever it is, is and stays yours. I'm just using it to build your site; I never claim ownership over it.

On my end: until your final payment clears, all the website files, code, and original copy I create stay mine. Once that final payment is in, everything I built transfers to you, full ownership, no strings attached.

08Expectations

I can't promise instant results, top search rankings, or a flood of traffic. This agreement covers building the site and the copy on it, that's it. I'm not responsible for how the site performs in search engines or AI results short or long-term.

I can promise that the website will be performance optimized, fully mobile responsive, and that it will be AA WCAG compliant. I also adhere to the AAA WCAG guidelines for contrast ratio to ensure content is easily legible, unless you specifically request a color combination that does not meet that standard.

Advice will be provided upon completion to keep it that way in case you intend to make changes on your own.

The Launch Package has no content management system, by design: nothing to update, nothing to break. Changes after handover (new text, photos, prices, hours) are made by me at the hourly rate below, usually in under an hour, or by any developer you choose, since the code is plain HTML.

Post-Launch Support: I provide a 14-day bug-fix period following site handover to fix any technical issues or broken features that fail to work as agreed in Section 2. Minor design modifications, new feature additions, or updates outside the original scope during or after this period will be billed at $60/hour.
