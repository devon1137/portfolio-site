Privacy Policy.

This site collects very little, and this page says exactly what. Short version: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking; the one form on the site is the service agreement, and what you type into it goes to me and nowhere else. The only affiliate links point to one product I made, and they're labeled.

Last updated September 15, 2026

Who This Covers

This policy covers the website you're reading, operated by Devon Kubacki, a freelance web developer and copywriter in Presque Isle, Maine. It doesn't cover client sites I've built (they have their own policies) or the external sites this one links to.

What Isn't Collected

  • No cookies. The site sets none, first-party or third-party.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no pixel, no tag manager, no session recording.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for, and no passwords are stored.
  • No third-party scripts. Fonts are system fonts; the stylesheet and script are served from this site. Nothing loads from an outside domain when a page opens. The one exception is opt-in: an embedded video that stays a local still image until you press play.

What Is Collected

The service agreement form

The services agreement is a form. If you fill it out and submit it, it collects what the agreement needs: your business name, email, phone number, printed name, a typed signature, the date, and your answers to the optional white-label and portfolio-opt-out choices. The form is handled by Netlify Forms, the hosting provider's form service, which stores the submission and emails it to me. That data is used to execute and keep a record of the agreement, and for nothing else. I keep signed agreements for as long as I need a record of the engagement, and in any case for the period the agreement itself describes.

Email

If you email me, I keep the correspondence like anyone keeps email. I don't add you to a list; there is no newsletter.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on Netlify. Like every web host, Netlify may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested, timestamps) for security and operations. I don't use those logs to identify visitors. Netlify's own handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.

Screenshots of Other Sites

The Work and Gallery pages show screenshots of websites I've built or worked on, captured from the live sites or from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine. Those screenshots are shown for portfolio purposes. Any names, logos, or trademarks in them belong to their owners.

Links to Other Sites

Links to client sites, the Wayback Machine, and other external pages open in a new tab and take you off this site. Their privacy practices are their own.

Affiliate Links

Devon Kubacki is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.

At the moment the only affiliate links on this site point to one product: the Adventure Log session notebook on the Notes of Yore case study, which is a product I designed. Each of those links is labeled as an affiliate link where it appears. If you buy through it, I may earn a small commission; the price you pay is the same either way. Following the link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon's own privacy notice applies and Amazon may set its own cookies to attribute the purchase. This site still sets none.

If more affiliate links are added later, each will carry a disclosure next to it and this section will be updated.

Embedded Videos

The Trail of Tales case study includes a YouTube video. It's built so that the page itself only shows a still image served from this site; no request goes to YouTube or Google, and no cookie is set, until you press play. If you do, YouTube's player loads from youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed mode) and from that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies to what it collects, which can include cookies once playback starts. Not pressing play means nothing is sent.

Your Choices

If you've submitted the agreement form or emailed me and want a copy of what I hold about you, want it corrected, or want it deleted, email me at devon.kubacki@gmail.com. I'll do it, unless a signed agreement means I need to keep a record for the term it covers, in which case I'll tell you that.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There's no mailing list to notify, so the page itself is the record.
