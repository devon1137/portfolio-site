Web Development

Custom WordPress and Shopify builds, from a two-person agency in 2015 to a hotel site proposed and built from behind the front desk.

  1. 2025–2026

    The Northeastland Hotel

    Website redesign and copywriting · WordPress

    Hired as front desk staff, I proposed and built a full redesign of the hotel's website from the ground up, with copy written to fit the company's mission and vision: 24 page templates and 6 conversion-focused landing pages with integrated booking, on a WordPress multisite install hosted on Cloudways.

    Matterport integration lets guests walk through rooms before they book, and a Trustindex review carousel does the trust-building work a boutique hotel needs.

    The rebuild cut the hotel's costs by more than $2,000 a year, and before I left I trained a coworker to own the site independently. The brief was "hyper-premium, but still feels locally owned," and every template and every line of copy was built around that.

  4. 2019–2021

    Streamershaven — Custom Theme

    Theme, brand, and logo design · WordPress

    Streamer's Haven was a tutorial site for live streamers that I launched in April 2019 and ran solo until I sold it. This entry is the build: I designed the brand, coded the custom WordPress theme, and built every template the site ran on.

    The design is dark with a single orange accent. The homepage sends new streamers to a beginner's guidebook, audio articles, and recommended tools through photo cards and a full-width guide banner. The article template is built for long reads: an auto-generated table of contents, slanted orange section headings, styled comparison tables, and a sidebar of latest and related posts. Underneath that: a member area with registration and profiles, a partnered-communities page, and affiliate and ad placements built into the layout rather than bolted on.

    Owning the theme meant every SEO fix the content side needed, from heading structure to page speed, was a change I could make myself. The logo is mine too, and I'm still proud of it: a webcam, an eye, and a Wi-Fi signal in one mark, for a streamer broadcasting to the viewers watching them.

SEO & Content

Research it, write it, optimize it, measure it. Classic and AI-assisted SEO tooling, with the HTML and schema knowledge to fix what the tools flag.

  1. 2019–2021

    Streamershaven

    Founder & publisher · content and SEO

    Streamer's Haven was a how-to site for live streamers, and for three years I was its entire editorial staff: 246 articles, every one researched, written, edited, illustrated, and optimized by me, writing under the pen name Mr. Goodhand.

    The content was organized around the questions people actually search: OBS Studio tutorials (audio filters, noise gates, 60fps settings, virtual camera), stream setup guides (internet speed, Discord servers, Twitch tools and channel points), hardware buying guides (microphones, headphones, capture cards, lighting, PC components), VST plugin walkthroughs, YouTube growth, platform news, and public-service pieces on account security and FTC disclosure rules. A beginner's guidebook tied the categories together for first-time streamers.

    Every piece started with keyword research and competitive analysis in Rank Math, Keywords Everywhere, SEMrush, and Ahrefs, and Google Search Console steered the content calendar toward the gaps. It grew to 40,000+ monthly visitors on organic search alone, no ad spend, on a custom theme I built myself, and I sold it in 2021.

    • Rank Math
    • SEMrush
    • Ahrefs
    • Search Console
    • Content strategy
    • WordPress
