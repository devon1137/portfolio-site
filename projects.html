  6. 2016

    The 2,700-Page Migration

    BrainandSpinalCord.org · Drupal to WordPress, by hand · Ardea Studios

    A full Drupal-to-WordPress migration for BrainandSpinalCord.org, a legal-resource site for injury survivors, moved into a new Divi build that reproduced the existing design one-for-one. The job was the content: 2,700+ pages and 3,400+ blog posts, imported individually rather than scripted, since the source structure didn't allow for a clean automated pass. About 800 hours of work, done over scope and over budget. We finished it. It's also the project that ended the company, since the time it took cost us the rest of our client list.

Sales

High-volume closing and consultative upselling, in English and conversational Spanish, with the numbers to show for it.

  1. 2024–2025

    Washville Car Wash

    Sales Associate · unlimited wash memberships

    Eight months in the pay booth at a tunnel wash averaging 600 cars a day, 1,000 in season. Closed 23.1% of the cars I pitched on an unlimited membership, with peak days over 50%: third in the company across multiple states, behind only two New York sites with a third more traffic. Sold 49 memberships on opening day, upsold the rest from the $10 base wash into $16–$25 packages, and learned the pitch in Spanish from the customers. The case study has the actual script.

Side Projects

Things built for the fun of it, on a deadline.

  1. 2020

    A Moon of Cheese

    GitHub Game Off 2020 · Unity stealth-platformer · artist and composer on a team of three

    A 2D pixel-art stealth platformer built in the jam month for the theme "Moonshot": you're a lab mouse the experiments made intelligent, and you're sneaking onto a rocket to reach the big ball of cheese in the sky. I did most of the art (the lab, the props, the rocket, the title screen) and all of the music, cutting royalty-free tracks into seamless loops in Reaper. Three of us, 351 commits in 30 days, 120 of them mine. Shipped for Windows, macOS, and Linux on itch.io, source on GitHub.
