A Build Log, Not a Highlight Reel.

Four kinds of work, one habit: ship it, then measure it. Web builds, SEO content, sales, and marketing operations, including the project that ended a company.

Web Development

Custom WordPress and Shopify builds, from a two-person agency in 2015 to a hotel site proposed and built from behind the front desk.

  1. 2025–2026

    The Northeastland Hotel

    Website redesign and copywriting · WordPress

    Hired as front desk staff, I proposed and built a full redesign of the hotel's website from the ground up, with copy written to fit the company's mission and vision: 24 page templates and 6 conversion-focused landing pages with integrated booking, on a WordPress multisite install hosted on Cloudways. Matterport integration lets guests walk through rooms before they book, and a Trustindex review carousel does the trust-building work a boutique hotel needs. The rebuild cut the hotel's costs by more than $2,000 a year, and before I left I trained a coworker to own the site independently. The brief was "hyper-premium, but still feels locally owned," and every template and every line of copy was built around that.

    Drupal to WordPress, by hand · Ardea Studios

About 800 hours of work, done over scope and over budget. We finished it. It's also the project that ended the company, since the time it took cost us the rest of our client list.

SEO & Content

Research it, write it, optimize it, measure it. Classic and AI-assisted SEO tooling, with the HTML and schema knowledge to fix what the tools flag.

  1. 2019–2021

    Streamershaven

    Founder & publisher · content, SEO, and the WordPress theme underneath it

    A content site I built, wrote, and ran alone: 246 articles on OBS Studio, Twitch setup, and streamer gear, on a custom WordPress theme I designed myself. Every piece was researched and optimized for search from the ground up, with Rank Math, Keywords Everywhere, SEMrush, and Ahrefs for keyword research, competitive analysis, and content-gap audits, and Google Search Console steering the content calendar. Grew it to 40,000+ monthly visitors through organic search alone, no ad spend, before selling it in 2021.

    • Rank Math
    • SEMrush
    • Ahrefs
    • Search Console
    • Content strategy
    • WordPress
