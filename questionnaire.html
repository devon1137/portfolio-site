Before I build

Website Questionnaire

Everything I need to design, build, and write your site, in one pass. Answer what you can and skip what you don't know; the gaps become my follow-up questions. It takes most people 30 to 45 minutes. Would you rather talk than type? Pick recorded call in Section 1 and I'll ask the same questions on a call instead, recorded so neither of us has to take notes.

02Your Business

The basics, in your own words. Don't polish anything; the way you'd explain it to a stranger at a barbecue is exactly what I want.

03What the Site Is For

A site can only do one thing well at a time, so I need to know which thing.

04Your Customers

I write to your customers, not to you, so I need to picture them.

06Your Pages

The Foundation Package includes up to six pages of your choosing, plus the blog framework, a Terms & Privacy page, and a sitemap page. Most sites use Home, About, Services, Contact, and two more: a second services page, testimonials, an FAQ, a portfolio or gallery.

The Launch Package includes up to four pages, and one of them is the Terms & Privacy page. Most sites use Home, About or Services, and Contact for the other three, with the FAQ folded into the Home or Services page.

07Words & Voice

I write the copy unless you're supplying it (the own-copy discount in the agreement). Either way, these shape it.

09Nuts & Bolts

The details the site plugs into, and the accounts around it.

Name, address, phone

Google checks your site against your Business Profile and every directory that lists you, and the match has to be exact: a missing suite number, "St" on one and "Street" on another, or a second phone number all count against you in local search. Give me these once, exactly as you want them everywhere, and I'll use them character for character.

Accounts and access

Don't send passwords here; I'll ask for access the safe way when it's needed.

10The Blog

The Foundation Package sets up the blog; writing the posts is on you (or a separate arrangement). A few questions so the framework fits how you'll actually use it.
