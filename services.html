The Launch Package

For a small business that needs a real website and doesn't want to spend a quarter managing the project. I design it, build it, and write the copy, so you don't have to supply anything but your logo, photos, and a conversation about what you do.

What you get is performance optimized, fully mobile responsive, and WCAG AA compliant, with AAA contrast so it's legible for everyone. It's built on WordPress, so you can add to it yourself afterward, and I'll leave you advice on how to keep it that way.

The whole arrangement is a four-page agreement written in plain English. You can read and sign it online right now. Don't need to edit the site yourself? The Foundation Package below is half the price.

How It Works

  1. Step 1

    Sign and Pay the Deposit

    Pick your package in the agreement, type your name to sign, and pay half the price as the deposit. That locks in your spot.

  2. Step 2

    Send Me Your Assets

    Logo, photos, brand materials, and anything you want on the site. The clock starts when everything's in.

  3. Step 3

    Concepts in a Week, or Two to Three

    Initial design and copy, delivered for your review: about a week for the Foundation Package, two to three for the Launch Package. You have five business days to send feedback on each round.

  4. Step 4

    Revisions

    One round on the Foundation Package, two on the Launch Package. Send each as one consolidated list. Copy, colors, spacing, image swaps, and minor layout adjustments are all in scope.

  5. Step 5

    Final Payment and Handover

    Pay the remaining half, and everything transfers to you: files, code, copy, full ownership. Then 14 days of bug-fix support.

Questions People Ask Before They Email

The ones I get most. If yours isn't here, ask it; the answer will probably end up on this list.

A week, or three weeks, from when exactly?

From the day I have your materials: logo, photos, the text you already have, and any brand rules. The deposit starts the project; the clock starts when the assets arrive. If you don't have photos or copy yet, tell me up front and we'll plan around it, but I can't write about a business I know nothing about.

Foundation Package or Launch Package: which one do I need?

Ask one question: will you add to the site yourself? If you'll write blog posts, add pages, or change prices every season, you want WordPress and the Launch Package. If the site needs to say who you are, what you do, and how to reach you, and then stay that way for a year or two, the Foundation Package does that for half the price, loads faster, and never needs an update. Most portfolios, trades, and single-location shops are Foundation Packages. If you're not sure, email me and I'll tell you which, honestly.

What does "built with AI assistance" mean?

It means I use AI tools the way I'd use any other: to draft, to generate the repetitive parts, and to check my work, which is what makes a one-week site possible at this price. It does not mean a template mill. I run the conversation with you, decide what goes on each page, write and edit the copy, design the layout, and check every page for speed and accessibility before you see it. The site you're reading was built the same way, and you can judge it for yourself.

I already have a website. Is this for me?

Usually, yes. Most Launch Package clients are replacing a site rather than starting from nothing, and the old site is useful: it tells me what's working, what isn't, and which pages already rank. If the site just needs specific fixes rather than a rebuild, say so and I'll quote those instead.

What platform do you build on, and will I be locked in?

The Launch Package is WordPress, because it's the platform your next developer is most likely to know, and Shopify when the site is a store. The Foundation Package is plain hand-coded HTML, which any developer can read and which has nothing to update. Either way it's standard tools, not a proprietary builder, and at handover you own the files, the code, the copy, and the hosting account. You could hire someone else the next day.

Who pays for hosting and the domain?

You do, directly, in your own name. I'll recommend a host, set it up, and move the site in, but the account and the bill are yours from the start, so nothing about your site depends on me staying in the picture. For a Foundation Package the hosting is free (Netlify's free tier is more than a small site needs), so the only recurring cost is the domain.

What happens after the 14-day bug-fix period?

The site keeps working. On a Launch Package, WordPress and plugin updates are yours to run, and I'll show you how at handover; on the Foundation Package there's nothing to update. If you'd rather not think about it, I offer maintenance by quote, and anything new (pages, features, content) is $60 an hour or a fixed quote, always with your written go-ahead first.

Will you write the words, or do I?

I write them; that's half of what either package is. You give me the facts (what you do, who for, what makes you different, the things customers always ask) and I turn that into pages. You review every word before it goes live, and copy tweaks are within the revision rounds.

Can you guarantee I'll rank on Google?

No, and I'd be wary of anyone who says yes. I build the site the way search engines want it built (fast, mobile-first, structured data, clean on-page SEO), and I've grown a site to 40,000 monthly readers doing exactly that. Rankings depend on your market and your competition as much as on the site.

You're in Maine. Does that matter?

Only if you want it to. Everything is done remotely, by email and calls, and the process is the same whether you're in Presque Isle or Phoenix. Local clients can meet in person; everyone else gets the same site.
