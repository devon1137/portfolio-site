How It Works

  1. Sign and Pay the Deposit

    Read the agreement, type your name to sign, and pay the $1,000 deposit. That locks in your spot.

  2. Send Me Your Assets

    Logo, photos, brand materials, and anything you want on the site. The clock starts when everything's in.

  3. Concepts in 2–3 Weeks

    Initial design and copy, delivered for your review. You have five business days to send feedback on each round.

  4. Two Rounds of Revisions

    Send each round as one consolidated list. Copy, colors, spacing, image swaps, and minor layout adjustments are all in scope.

  5. Final Payment and Handover

    Pay the remaining $1,000, and everything transfers to you: files, code, copy, full ownership. Then 14 days of bug-fix support.
