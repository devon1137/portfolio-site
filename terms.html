Terms of Service.

The standard terms for my freelance web development and copywriting work, in plain language. Every project also gets a signed agreement; where the two differ, the signed agreement wins. The last section covers use of this site itself.

Last updated September 14, 2026

1. Who and What

"I," "me," and "the Developer" mean Devon Kubacki, a sole-proprietor freelance web developer and copywriter in Presque Isle, Maine. "You" and "the Client" mean the person or business hiring me. These terms apply to freelance services I provide, including the Launch Package. Each project is governed by a written agreement we both sign (the current one is here); these terms describe what that agreement normally contains, and fill in anything it doesn't say.

2. Scope

The work covered is what the signed agreement lists, and nothing else. Anything outside that list (extra pages, new features, ongoing maintenance, work on other sites) is a separate quote or billed hourly at the rate in the agreement, with your written go-ahead first.

3. Payment

  • Fixed-price projects are billed 50% at signing and 50% at completion. No work starts before the deposit is received.
  • The final balance is due before the site is transferred to your domain or handed over, regardless of how early the build finishes.
  • Hourly work outside the fixed scope is billed at the rate stated in the agreement (currently $60/hour) and invoiced as it's done.
  • Third-party costs (hosting, domain registration, premium plugins or themes, paid APIs, stock photography) are yours and are billed to you directly by those providers.

4. Revisions and Approval

Fixed-price packages include two rounds of revisions. Each round is one consolidated list of changes. Revisions cover copy tweaks, colors, spacing, image swaps, and minor repositioning within the agreed layout; structural redesigns or scope changes after layout approval are billed separately. You have 5 business days to review each submission; if I hear nothing in that window, the work is treated as approved and the project moves on.

5. Timeline

The production clock starts when I have everything I need from you: photos, logo, written content, brand materials, and access. If your materials are more than 30 consecutive days late, the project goes on hold and a restart fee may apply. If I expect to miss a deadline, I'll tell you before it, not after.

6. Ownership

Everything you give me (logo, photos, copy, brand assets) is and stays yours. Everything I create (files, code, original copy) stays mine until your final payment clears, then transfers to you in full, no strings. I may reuse general techniques, snippets, and know-how on other projects; I won't reuse your specific content or design.

7. What I Promise, and What I Don't

I promise a site that is performance-optimized, fully mobile-responsive, and WCAG 2 AA compliant, with AAA contrast unless you specifically ask for a color combination that doesn't meet it. I include a 14-day bug-fix period after handover for anything that doesn't work as agreed.

I don't promise search rankings, traffic, leads, or revenue. Building the site and writing the copy is the job; how search engines and AI systems treat it afterward is outside my control.

8. Confidentiality and Portfolio

Anything confidential either of us shares during a project stays between us. I'll sign an NDA if you need one. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, I may show the finished site and describe the project in my portfolio and promotional material after launch; you can opt out in the agreement or by requesting an NDA before signing.

9. Cancellation

  • If you cancel: the deposit is retained; it covers work already done and isn't refundable.
  • If I cancel: you get the deposit back minus a flat fee stated in the agreement for work done to that point.
  • Either way: your assets remain yours; I keep copies for 90 days in case you need them, then delete them. Work-in-progress that was never paid for in full stays mine.

10. Liability

To the maximum extent the law allows, my total liability for anything arising from a project is capped at the amount you actually paid me for it. I'm not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including lost profits or revenue, or for downtime, data loss, or failures caused by third-party hosting or services.

11. Communication

Email is the reliable channel. Phone hours are 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern, Monday through Friday, and even then I may not pick up on the first ring.

12. Governing Law

These terms and every agreement under them are governed by the laws of the State of Maine.

13. Using This Website

The text, design, and code of this site are mine. Screenshots of client and past-project sites are shown for portfolio purposes; the names, logos, and trademarks in them belong to their owners. The Launch Package price and details on the Services page are an invitation to get in touch, not a binding offer; the signed agreement is the contract. See the Privacy Policy for what the site collects. Links to outside sites are provided as-is, and I'm not responsible for their content. Where a link is an affiliate link, it says so next to the link and in the Privacy Policy's Affiliate Links section.
