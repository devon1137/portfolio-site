The Brief

BrainandSpinalCord.org is a legal-resource site for brain and spinal cord injury survivors, run by a law firm: hundreds of medical explainer pages, a state-by-state attorney directory, a rehab directory, and a long-running blog. It was on Drupal 7. The client wanted the WordPress back end, and wanted the site to look exactly as it did.

That was the whole brief. Not a redesign. A migration that nobody visiting the site would notice.

What I Did

Rebuilding the design one-for-one

I rebuilt the Drupal theme in Divi: the teal header, the white nav bar with the green search block, the MRI hero on every inner page, the section sidebar, the state grid, the case-evaluation form. Put the March 2016 capture next to the November one and the difference is the logo rendering. That was the point.

Moving the content by hand

The source structure didn't allow a clean automated import, so the content came over one item at a time: 2,700+ pages and 3,400+ blog posts, each placed into the right template with its images, links, and sidebar assignments intact. Roughly 800 hours of work, over scope and over budget.

What it cost

We finished it. It's also the project that ended Ardea Studios: the time it took cost us the rest of our client list. I put it in the portfolio anyway, because it's the most honest thing here about what scope does to a two-person shop, and because the site went live and stayed live.
