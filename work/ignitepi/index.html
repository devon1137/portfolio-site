The Brief

Ignite Presque Isle is the 501(c)(3) that owns The Northeastland and runs the Innovation Center inside it: a co-working space, a leadership development series, and a network of regional partners for Aroostook County. While I was rebuilding the hotel site, it became obvious the nonprofit needed one too, and that it should share infrastructure rather than be a second, separate build to maintain.

Same multisite install, same theme family, different job. A hotel site exists to convert a visitor into a booking. A nonprofit site exists to explain what the organization does, prove it's legitimate, and make it easy to get involved or donate. The structure follows from that.

What I Did

Structure for a nonprofit

The homepage walks through mission, programs, partners, and impact in that order, with the metrics the board actually reports (professionals trained, businesses supported, partners, events, grants) laid out as a list rather than buried in prose. Program cards for the Innovation Center and Ignite You Leadership Development each get a photo, a plain description, and one call to action.

Public documentation

A 501(c)(3) is held to a public-accessibility standard a hotel isn't. The documentation page publishes the articles of incorporation, the IRS determination letter, and Form 990 filings in expandable sections, so anyone doing due diligence on the organization can find what they need without asking.

Shared work with the hotel

The same image-optimization pass that ran across the hotel site ran here: WebP, under 100 KB each. The two sites share hosting, updates, and the person maintaining them, which was the point of building them together.
